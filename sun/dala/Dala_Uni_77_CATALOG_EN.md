# Dala Üni — Voice of the Steppe · catalog of all 77 tones (English)

Original music by AISun for SUN (Astana, Kazakhstan). Every tone is synthesised from code, one instrument voice per tone.

**What's in the archive:** 51 ringtones (27 s each) and 26 message tones (1.4–1.8 s), every tone in three formats — MP3 (192 kbps), M4R (iPhone) and OGG (Android). File names below match the files in the archive.

How to set a ringtone on iPhone or Android: see README_Dala_Uni.txt in the archive.

## The series
- **TAN — Dawn.** Dombra at sunrise, an even, calm rhythm.
- **TULPAR — The gallop.** Dombra on the tökpe horse-gallop rhythm, with bells.
- **SAMAL — The breeze.** Sybyzgy, the Kazakh wooden flute.
- **KOKZHIEK — The horizon.** Kobyz, the ancient bowed instrument — a prayer for the descendants.
- **SMS_BELL / SMS_DOMBRA / SMS_SHANKOBYZ — short messages:** bells, dombra, and shankobyz (jaw harp).
- **SUN — 5 signature tones** from our product page, including "The steppe wind" on sybyzgy.
