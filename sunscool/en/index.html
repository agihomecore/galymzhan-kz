SUNScool — AI in the classroom, no internet

The school's own artificial intelligence: the server stays in the school, students use the AI on their own computers, and children's questions and answers never leave for the internet.

Қазақша · Русский Runs on the school network The teacher runs the class School data stays in school

How it works

One installer, three roles. At install you choose: 1 — Server, 2 — Teacher seat, 3 — Student seat.

Student seatA student's computer. No model installed — it connects to the server. Kids mode: the lesson only, no outside websites.
⇄
SUNScool serverA dedicated computer in the server room or the computer lab. The AI model runs here. Switched on before lessons start.
⇄
Teacher seatThe teacher's computer. No model installed — it connects to the server. Helps prepare lessons, tasks and explanations.

Who it is for

Students

A helper in the lesson

Explains a topic in plain words in Kazakh or Russian, checks understanding, helps with texts. Kids mode: lesson tasks only, no outside websites.

Teachers

Class panel

Tasks, lesson progress, what students asked. The teacher decides when the AI is on. For lesson prep the teacher has an AI helper of their own.

Schools

No cloud, no per-student subscriptions

One server per school. Children's personal data never leaves the school — as Kazakhstan's personal data law requires.

Safety

Separate networks

Student computers and the teacher's workplace are kept apart.

AI label

AI-made texts are labelled — as Kazakhstan's AI law requires.

The teacher decides

The AI helps but never grades or decides for the teacher.
